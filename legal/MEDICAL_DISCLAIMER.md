# Medical disclaimer

**Heartline is not a medical device.** It is a general wellness app for adults. It has not been
cleared or approved by the FDA or any other health authority.

- It **cannot diagnose** atrial fibrillation or any other condition, and it cannot detect a heart
  attack, stroke, blood clot or other emergency.
- ECG rhythm labels, blood pressure estimates and every other value are **estimates** that can
  be wrong. Blood pressure is estimated from an optical sensor and must be checked with a
  validated cuff.
- **Don't change your medication or treatment** because of Heartline. Talk to a health
  professional about any result or symptom.
- **If you think you are having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number now.**

See the full [Terms of Use](TERMS_OF_USE.md).
