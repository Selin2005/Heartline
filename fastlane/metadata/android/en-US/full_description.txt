Heartline brings heart health features to your Galaxy Watch and keeps your history on your phone, in a clean One UI style.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Record a 30-second single-lead ECG with your watch and see a wellness rhythm label: sinus rhythm, signs of AFib, high or low heart rate, inconclusive or poor recording.
• View every ECG on ECG paper and save it as a PDF to share with your doctor.
• Estimate blood pressure with your watch after calibrating it with your own cuff.
• Turn on background checks for an irregular rhythm and high or low heart rate.
• Check blood oxygen, skin temperature, stress and body composition.
• Follow trends, daily goals and a weekly summary.
• Use widgets on the phone and tiles and complications on the watch.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data stays on your watch and phone. There is no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. Data only leaves your devices when you export or share it.

FREE SOFTWARE
Heartline is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0. The source code is at github.com/selin2005/heartline.

REQUIREMENTS
A Galaxy Watch4 or newer, paired with an Android phone. Install Heartline on both.

IMPORTANT
Heartline is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it cannot detect a heart attack, stroke or other emergency. Blood pressure values are estimates. Don't change your medication or treatment based on Heartline. If you think you are having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.
