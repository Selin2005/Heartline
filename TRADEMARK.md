# Heartline name and logo policy

The Heartline **source code** is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The Heartline
**name, logo, app icon and visual identity** ("the Marks") are not covered by that license. They
identify the official app published by the Maintainer (Selin) from
<https://github.com/selin2005/heartline>.

## You may
- Say that your project is "based on Heartline" or "a fork of Heartline", in plain text.
- Use the Marks to refer to the official Heartline project, for example in articles, reviews,
  tutorials or links to the official repository and store listings.
- Build and run unmodified official releases for yourself.

## You may not, without written permission
- Publish, on any app store, website or other distribution channel, an app or build that uses
  the name "Heartline", a confusingly similar name, the Heartline logo or icon, or the official
  application ID (`io.github.selin2005.heartline`, or the older `com.heartline.app`).
- Suggest that your fork, service or product is official, endorsed or supported by the Heartline
  project.
- Use the Marks in a domain name, company name, product name or social media handle.

## If you distribute a fork
You must, in addition to complying with the AGPL:
1. choose a clearly different name and icon;
2. change the `applicationId` of both the phone app and the watch app;
3. remove or replace the update check that points to the official GitHub releases;
4. state clearly that it is an unofficial fork and link to the original project.

We'd much rather have your improvements in the official app. Please consider sending them as a
pull request (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

For permission requests, open an issue or contact the Maintainer through GitHub.
